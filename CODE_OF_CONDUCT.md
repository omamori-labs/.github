# Code of conduct

Omamori is a small project made by people who like osu!. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in omamori-labs, is expected to keep it a good place to be.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to modding, C# or open source.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Assume good intent.
- Respect people's time

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personalattacks.
- Sexual content.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Using Omamori to cheat, or helping others to.
- Anything else a reasonable person would see as making the space unsafe or unwelcome.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code to **catforgor@proton.me**. Reports are read only by the maintainers and kept private. If a maintainer is involved, say so and they'll be left out of handling it.
